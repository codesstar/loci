# decisions/ — Decision Records

Important decisions with context, options considered, and reasoning.

Research material does not belong here. Raw docs, competitive notes, study guides, market scans, and case studies are evidence; store them in `references/research/` and cite them from a decision record when they support a choice.

## File Naming
`YYYY-MM-DD-short-description.md`

## Template Structure
Every decision should include:
1. **Background** — Why this decision was needed
2. **Options** — What was considered
3. **Decision** — What was chosen and why
4. **Follow-up** — Action items resulting from the decision

## After Writing

Run the L1 promotion check: if this decision changes current strategy, principles, priorities, or project state, update the smallest current surface (`plan.md`, `tasks/active.md` via the guarded task writer, `projects/index.md`, or the relevant project `.loci/memory.md`). If it does not change current behavior, leave it as L3 history only.

## Why Record Decisions?
- Future you will forget why you chose X over Y
- Prevents re-litigating the same decision
- Shows your thinking evolution over time
