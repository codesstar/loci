# Wellbeing

## Body

- Running 2x/week, want 3x. Signed up for the Portland Marathon (half) in October.
- Knees get tight when I ramp mileage too fast — warm up properly, don't skip it.
- Cold brew is a crutch after 2pm and it wrecks my sleep. Cutoff is noon.

## Sleep & Energy

- Best sleep when screens are off by 10pm. Ship late and scroll after, and I'm foggy till lunch.
- Energy peaks 7-11am — that's why deep work lives there. Afternoons are for meetings and admin, not hard problems.
- Two bad nights in a row and my judgment on the product gets shaky. Sleep is a work input, not a reward.

## Mental State & Confidence

- I overthink when I'm anxious — usually right before a launch or an invoice. Naming it helps; shipping something small helps more.
- Confidence tracks momentum, not outcomes. A daily commit streak does more for my head than any single win.
- Comparing myself to other founders is the fastest way to feel behind. Close the tab, ship the thing.

## Principles

- State > productivity. One rested day of focused work beats three wired days of thrashing.
- Protect the morning. It's the one block that reliably produces my best work.
