# Insights

Fresh reflections as they happen. Some harden into values or lessons; some expire. Each keeps enough context to make sense later.

## 2026-03-11 — Simplicity keeps showing up in my decisions

**背景 / Background**: Looked back at the last few decisions — niche-down, the tech-stack choice, pricing. Every one was about cutting, not adding.
**洞察 / Insight**: My best calls lately all move toward *fewer, better* things. When I add scope, it's usually anxiety, not strategy.
**为什么重要 / Why it matters**: If simplification is the pattern that works for me, I should reach for it on purpose instead of stumbling into it.
**暂定影响 / Tentative impact**: Before adding anything — a feature, a client, a side project — ask "what would I cut instead?"
**状态 / Status**: observing

## 2026-03-08 — Morning attention is my real currency

**背景 / Background**: The days that feel "mine" are the ones where I protected the 7-11am block; the fragmented ones started with Twitter.
**洞察 / Insight**: I run on morning attention. When it scatters early, the whole day feels less like mine — it's identity fuel, not just productivity.
**为什么重要 / Why it matters**: Explains why a small morning habit swings my whole day and mood, not just my output.
**暂定影响 / Tentative impact**: Guard the first two hours. No feeds before deep work.
**状态 / Status**: promoted-to-learned
